## QuickTerm 1.6.16

Universal binary (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 15.4+), signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Arrives through the updater.

### New
- **`Cmd+F` finds inside the pane.** In a terminal, a find bar opens in the pane's top-right corner and every match is highlighted; Return goes to the next match, Shift+Return to the previous one, and both wrap around at the ends. In a browser pane it finds in the current page. Esc closes the bar and clears the highlight.
- **A zoomed pane says so.** A translucent shrink icon sits in its top-right corner; click it to un-zoom.
- **Swapping panes slides.** `Cmd+Shift+←↑↓→` now moves the two panes to each other's place, scaling when their sizes differ. `swap-animation-duration` sets the length (default 0.5 s, 0 turns it off).

### Changed
- **Zoom moved to `Cmd+Shift+F`**, so that `Cmd+F` can be find, as in every other app.
- **Scrolling layout: a column peeking in at the screen edge takes a click.** Moving the mouse across it no longer slides the whole strip; hover focus works as before everywhere else.
- **Scrolling layout: a two-finger swipe is one column.** It brings in the column hidden past that edge and focuses it, and the strip never stops halfway between columns.

### Diagnostics
- The diagnostic log (`diagnostic-log = true`) now names the pane, screen and workspace of every agent state change, and the process lineage behind every hook. This is to track down a report of one pane's agent status showing on another pane; a line marked `MISMATCH` is what to send.
