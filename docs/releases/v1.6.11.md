## QuickTerm 1.6.11

Universal binary (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 15.4+), signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Arrives through the updater.

### Fixes
- **The grab hand appears the moment `⌘` is pressed over a tiled pane, even when the pointer is standing still.** The hand came from the drag overlay's cursor rect alone, which only takes effect once the window re-evaluates its rects, and nothing asked for that when the overlay appeared under a stationary pointer. A trackpad never quite stands still, so the development MacBook never showed the gap; with a mouse on a Mac mini the pane could be dragged (since 1.6.10) but the cursor never changed. The overlay now sets the cursor itself when it appears and when the pointer enters it, and hands it back when `⌘` is released.
