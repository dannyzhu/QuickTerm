## QuickTerm 1.6.14

Universal binary (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 15.4+), signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Arrives through the updater.

### Fixes
- **The grab hand appears over every tiled pane the moment `⌘` is pressed, with a mouse that stays put as much as with a trackpad.** Three releases tried to set the hand from AppKit — a cursor rect, a cursor-update tracking area, setting the cursor directly — and every one of them was undone by SwiftUI, which owns the cursor inside its own view hierarchy and re-applies the terminal's I-beam on its next pass; a trackpad's constant tiny movements kept re-triggering the hand, which is why the gap never showed on a MacBook, while a mouse that stands still left the I-beam in place, except over whichever pane's overlay happened to mount last. The overlay now tells SwiftUI its pointer style directly: the open hand while `⌘` is held, the closed hand while dragging, the link pointer over a link. Reproduced and verified in a scratch window before shipping.
