## QuickTerm 1.6.10

Universal binary (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 15.4+), signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Arrives through the updater.

### Fixes
- **`⌘`+drag of a tiled pane works on a Mac that does not have Ghostty installed.** The pasteboard type behind the drag was Ghostty's own identifier, kept from the port without being declared in QuickTerm's Info.plist. On a Mac with Ghostty.app installed, Ghostty's declaration made Launch Services know the type and everything worked; on a Mac without it the type was unknown, every drop target refused the drag, and holding `⌘` over a pane did nothing — while dragging a floating pane, which never touches the pasteboard, kept working. QuickTerm now declares a type of its own.

### Diagnostics
- The `modifiers` log category added in 1.6.9 now also covers the drag itself: the mouse-down on the drag overlay, the moment the drag threshold is crossed, the drag session beginning and ending, and a `⌘`+click that a floating pane claims before the tiled pane underneath could see it.
