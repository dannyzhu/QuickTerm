## QuickTerm 1.6.13

Universal binary (Apple Silicon + Intel, macOS 15.4+), signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Arrives through the updater.

### Fixes
- **The grab hand stays on the pane under a pointer that does not move.** The hand is a cursor rect of the drag overlay, and the terminal's I-beam is one too (the scroll view's document cursor). AppKit lets the most recently rebuilt of two overlapping rects win, so when the second pane's overlay appeared a millisecond after the first, the terminal's I-beam took the pane under the pointer back — invisible on a trackpad, whose slightest motion restores the hand, but permanent with a mouse that stays put: on a Mac mini the hand showed over one pane of a split and not the other. While `⌘` is held the terminal now registers no cursor of its own, so the hand is the only cursor over the pane, and the terminal's cursor returns the moment `⌘` is released.
